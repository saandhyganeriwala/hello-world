Google Sheets is free, familiar and shared by default. For many small teams it's the obvious place to keep orders, payments and leads. So when does it make sense to use something else?

Here's a straight comparison between Google Sheets and [CRUD.chat](/), including where Sheets is the better choice.

## The short version

- **Choose Google Sheets** if your team is comfortable with spreadsheets, mostly works at a computer, and needs formulas, custom calculations or financial models.
- **Choose CRUD.chat** if your team records things on the go from phones, doesn't want to maintain formulas, and needs different views (pipelines, calendars, ledgers, charts) for different kinds of records.

Many teams use both: CRUD.chat for day-to-day records, and Sheets for occasional analysis on an exported CSV.

## Where Google Sheets wins

**Flexibility.** Any calculation you can imagine, any layout, any formula. If your process depends on custom maths, such as pricing calculators, commission rules or financial projections, a spreadsheet is the right tool.

**Cost.** It's free. For a solo user who's happy at a laptop, that's hard to beat.

**Familiarity.** Almost everyone has used a spreadsheet. There's nothing new to learn.

**Ecosystem.** Add-ons, scripts and integrations for almost anything.

## Where CRUD.chat wins

**Recording from the field.** A salesperson in a shop or a manager at a site can record an order in five seconds by typing "Order from Kashish 45 Samayi at 500 each" or sending a voice note. The fields are filled in for them. This is the main reason teams switch: records that used to be entered late, or never, get entered as they happen.

**Consistent data without rules.** Amounts, dates and names come out structured the same way no matter how people phrase things, so totals add up.

**The right view for each job.** Orders on a board by status, leads in a pipeline with totals, leaves on a calendar, payments in a ledger with a running balance, all from the same records and without building anything.

**Answers without formulas.** "How much is pending from Hotel Orchid?" or "top customers this month" get answered from your data. No SUMIFs.

**An interface that changes when you ask.** Need a chart of sales by product, or a water-intake tracker for the staff challenge? Ask for it and it appears.

## Switching without losing anything

CRUD.chat imports Excel and CSV files, so you can bring your existing sheets in. It exports any section to CSV, so you can always take your data back to a spreadsheet for analysis.
