Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

In short: we collect what we need to run CRUD.chat — your login, what you record and basic usage data. We don’t sell it, we don’t show ads, and we use trusted providers (for AI, voice transcription, email, payments and hosting) only to deliver the Service. You can export or delete your data.

1. Who is responsible

Crud, 903, Raheja Willows, Lokhandwala, Kandivali East, Mumbai 400101, Maharashtra, India, operates CRUD.chat and is responsible for your personal data under this policy and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address or phone number, your name, and the workspaces you belong to.
  • Your Content: messages, voice notes, screenshots, files you import and the records created from them, including any details about customers, staff or others that you choose to record.
  • Billing: your plan, subscription and payment status, and Razorpay payment and subscription IDs. Card, UPI and bank details are collected and stored by Razorpay, not by us.
  • Usage and device data: AI actions used and their cost, log data such as IP address, browser type and timestamps, used for security, debugging and fair-use limits.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the Service: log you in, turn what you record into structured records, answer your questions, transcribe voice notes, and sync your workspace across your team.
  • To bill Pro subscriptions and enforce plan limits.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To send essential messages, such as login codes, receipts and changes to these policies. We do not send marketing email without your consent.

We do not sell your personal data or use Your Content for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

We use these service providers, only to deliver the Service and under their own confidentiality and security commitments:

  • Anthropic (AI models that understand messages, screenshots and questions).
  • OpenAI or Sarvam AI (voice note transcription, when enabled).
  • Brevo (sending login codes by email).
  • Razorpay (payments and subscriptions).
  • Railway (hosting and database).

Some of these providers process data outside India. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public. Other members of a workspace can see the content recorded in it.

5. Cookies and storage

We use a single essential cookie to keep you logged in, and your browser’s local storage for preferences such as your last open view. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account and Your Content while your account is active. When you delete records they are removed from the workspace; when you ask us to delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except billing records we must keep under tax and accounting law (generally up to 8 years). Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to production systems is restricted. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

8. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to exercise these rights for you. You can delete your account at any time in the app (menu → Delete account) — see how account deletion works. Most of the rest can be done in the app; for anything else, write to login@crud.chat. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Children

CRUD.chat is a business tool and is not meant for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect their personal data.

10. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy here and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or by email.
