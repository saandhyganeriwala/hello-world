Shipping & delivery

Last updated 11 November 2026

In short: CRUD.chat is an online software service. Nothing is shipped — you get access instantly, in your browser.

How the Service is delivered

  • CRUD.chat is delivered entirely online at crud.chat. No physical goods are sold or shipped.
  • The free plan is available as soon as you log in.
  • Pro is activated on your workspace immediately after a successful payment, usually within seconds. You will see the Pro badge in the app and receive a payment confirmation from Razorpay.

If access isn’t activated

If Pro is not active within one hour of a successful payment, write to login@crud.chat or call +91 70037 05584 with your login email or phone number and the Razorpay payment ID. We will activate it or refund the payment.

Where it’s available

The Service can be used from any country where we accept payment and where its use is lawful. It works on any modern phone or computer browser.
