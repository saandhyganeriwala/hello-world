Delete your CRUD.chat account

Last updated 12 November 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open CRUD.chat (the app, or crud.chat/app) and log in.
  2. Tap the ⋯ menu at the top right.
  3. Choose Delete account, type DELETE and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

By email

If you can’t log in, write to login@crud.chat from the email address you log in with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete accounts within 7 days of a verified request and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your login (email or phone number), name and sessions.
  • Workspaces that only you are in, with all of their records, messages and files, and any subscription on them is cancelled.
  • Your membership of shared workspaces.

What is kept

  • Workspaces you share with teammates stay with them, including records you added (they show the name you used). If you owned one, the longest-standing teammate becomes the owner.
  • Payment records required by tax and accounting law, for up to 8 years.
  • Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis within 30 days.

See our Privacy policy for details.
