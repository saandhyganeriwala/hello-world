Cancellation & refund policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

In short: cancel any time and you won’t be charged again. Pro stays on until the end of the month you’ve paid for. Once a paid month has started it is not refunded — except for duplicate charges or our mistakes, which we refund in full.

Cancelling your subscription

  • Open CRUD.chat, tap the plan chip at the top (or Menu → Plan & usage) and choose Cancel subscription. You can also cancel by writing to login@crud.chat from the email you log in with; we process these within one working day.
  • Cancellation stops all future charges. The workspace keeps Pro until the end of the current paid month, then moves to the free plan.
  • Nothing is deleted when Pro ends. Your records, views and members stay; only the free plan’s limits apply.
  • You can also cancel the mandate from your bank or UPI app. Please cancel in CRUD.chat too, so our records match.

Refunds

No refunds once a paid period has started. Subscription payments are for a month of service in advance and are not refunded, in whole or in part, after that month begins — including for unused days, unused AI actions or a downgrade part-way through a month.

We will refund in full:

  • a duplicate charge for the same month;
  • a charge made after you cancelled, because of an error on our side;
  • any other charge made in error by us.

To ask for one of these, write to login@crud.chat within 30 days of the charge with your login email or phone number and the Razorpay payment ID. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 working days; your bank may take a few more days to show it.

Failed or pending payments

If money was debited but the payment failed or Pro did not activate, it is usually reversed automatically by your bank or Razorpay within 5–7 working days. If it isn’t, write to us and we will sort it out.

Free plan

The free plan costs nothing and needs no cancellation.
