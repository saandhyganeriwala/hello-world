## Version page (Distribution → iOS App → 1.0)

**Promotional text (170):**
Record orders, payments, leads and tasks by typing, a voice note or a screenshot. CRUD.chat turns them into a shared app for your team. No spreadsheets, no formulas.

**Description (4000):**
CRUD.chat is the simplest way for a small team to keep its business records, without spreadsheets.

Record anything the way you'd tell a colleague: type a message, send a voice note or share a screenshot. "Order from Kashish, 45 boxes at 500 each, deliver Hyderabad" becomes an order with the customer, quantity, amount and delivery filled in. A screenshot of a customer's message or a bill is read for you.

EVERYTHING IN ITS PLACE
• Sections such as Orders, Payments, Leads, Tasks and Inventory appear automatically from what you record
• See each section the way that suits it: list, table, pipeline board, calendar, chart, ledger or tracker
• Edit any record right where you see it
• Import your Excel or CSV files, and export to CSV any time

JUST ASK
• "How much is pending from Hotel Orchid?" or "Top customers this month?", answered from your own records
• Change the app by asking: "show leads as a pipeline", "add a chart of sales by product", or describe any view you want and CRUD.chat builds it

BUILT FOR TEAMS
• Invite your team with a link; everyone sees changes instantly
• Messages in English or Hinglish
• Works on iPhone, iPad and in any browser at crud.chat

PLANS
• Free: 10 AI actions per person to try it, up to 3 people in a workspace. Viewing, editing and searching are always free.
• CRUD.chat Pro (auto-renewing monthly subscription): up to 10 people in the workspace and about 500 AI actions a month (fair use).

Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically each month unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID account settings.

- **Notes:**

> CRUD.chat is a team record-keeping app for small businesses. To sign in: enter the email above, tap Continue, then enter the 6-digit code above as the login code (no email is needed for this demo account).
>
> What to try:
> 1. Type a message such as "Order from Ravi, 10 boxes at 500 each, paid" and tap send: it becomes a structured order record.
> 2. Tap the microphone to record a voice note (it is transcribed into the message box), or the image button to read a screenshot (camera and photo library).
> 3. Switch views with the view picker (List, Table, Board…) and tap any record to edit it in place.
> 4. Ask a question, e.g. "How many orders do we have?".
